The side panel can now hold up to four groups, side by side or stacked, each with its own tabs. New tabs open in the group you used last.

## Shortcuts

- **⌘\\** splits right, **⌘⌥↓** splits down (Ctrl instead of ⌘ on Windows)
- **⌘⌥→** and **⌘⌥←** move between groups
- **⌘⌥⇧→** moves the current tab to the next group
- **⌘⇧W** closes a group, and the others fill the space

## Stays where you left it

- Drag tabs between groups, or drag in files, tickets and pull requests
- Your panel stays as it is when you switch rooms or projects, and comes back after a restart
- On a small window the groups stack, and a panel covering the page steps aside when you open another page
